Travel & stay

Everything that keeps the artists rested, fed and on time for your event. All items below are provided and fully covered by the Organizer. Please confirm all points ahead of the booking.

01 · Traveling party
✦2 drummers + 1 manager - 3 people traveling as one group
02 · Accommodation & meals
✦Hotel: 4-star or above, 2 double rooms, fully covered by the Organizer
✦Meals: full hot meal coverage for the traveling party on performance day(s)
03 · Ground transportation
✦Local logistics: the Organizer covers all ground transfers - Airport ↔ Hotel ↔ Venue / Soundcheck / Show
04 · Air travel
✦Flights: round-trip airfare for all 3 travelers, arranged and fully covered by the Organizer
✦Baggage: the Organizer also covers checked baggage fees for percussion equipment and personal luggage

At the venue

What the venue needs to have ready before the artists arrive.

01A private green room / dressing room for the traveling party (3 people), with a mirror, seating, good lighting, and space to change and rest
02Drinking water (still) available at all times during soundcheck and performance
03A selection of fresh fruits and fresh juices
04Tea and coffee available backstage
05Clean towels
06Access to a clean restroom near the performance or backstage area
07Secure space to leave personal belongings
